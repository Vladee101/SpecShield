# Glossary

A shipment is booked in a single block and priced by the square metre. The final
segment of a route is the last leg before delivery. Choosing a lighter pallet is
a wise trade when the volume is low.

Rates are amplitude-independent, and the paddle wheel on a barge does not change
them. A clerk at the depot signs for the load.

The stack is React, Postgres, Prisma, Docker, and Kubernetes.
